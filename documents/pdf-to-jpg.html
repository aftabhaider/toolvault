    How to use PDF to JPG Converter

    1. Choose or drop a PDF.
    2. Pick JPG or PNG and a resolution: 150 DPI suits screens and email, 300 DPI suits printing.
    3. Select Convert to images. One page downloads as an image; several pages download as a ZIP.

    Resolution

    DPI is pixels per inch of the printed page. An A4 page at 150 DPI is about 1240 × 1754 pixels; at 300 DPI it is about 2480 × 3508 pixels.

    Text and fonts

    Pages are rendered exactly as a PDF viewer shows them. The images contain pictures of the text, not editable text; use PDF to Word if you need to edit it.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert a PDF to JPG for free?

    Choose your PDF, pick JPG and a resolution, and select Convert to images. There is no sign-up or watermark.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Pages are rendered in your browser with Mozilla’s pdf.js. The file stays on your device.

    Which resolution should I use?

    150 DPI for viewing on screen or sending by email; 300 DPI for printing or zooming into detail. 72 DPI gives the smallest files.

    Can I convert only one page?

    Yes. Choose “Selected pages” and type the page number, for example 3, or a range such as 2-4.
