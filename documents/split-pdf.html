Split PDF

Extract the pages you need into a new PDF, or split a document into single pages or equal-sized parts. Everything happens on your device.

Drop a PDF hereor
    How to split
    Pages to extractExample: 1-3, 5 makes one PDF with pages 1, 2, 3 and 5, in that order.
    Pages per file

    Private: files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

    How to use Split PDF

    1. Choose or drop a PDF.
    2. Pick how to split it: extract pages into one new PDF, save every page as its own PDF, or split into parts with a fixed number of pages.
    3. Select Split PDF. Several files download together as a ZIP.

    Extract pages

    Type pages and ranges separated by commas. “8-” means page 8 to the end; “-3” means the first three pages. Pages appear in the order you type them, so “3, 1, 2” also reorders.

    Quality

    Pages are copied, not re-rendered, so text stays selectable and images keep their original quality.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I extract pages from a PDF?

    Choose “Extract pages”, type the pages you want (for example 2-4, 7) and select Split PDF. You get one new PDF with just those pages.

    How do I split a PDF into single pages?

    Choose “Every page”. Each page becomes its own PDF, downloaded together in a ZIP file.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The split is done in your browser with pdf-lib; the file never leaves your device.

    Can I delete pages from a PDF with this?

    Yes: extract every page except the ones you want to remove. For a 10-page file without page 4, type 1-3, 5-10.
