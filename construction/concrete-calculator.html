Concrete Calculator Formulas

Every element is converted to metres first. Geometry is calculated from the corresponding shape formula, the individual element quantities are added, and the waste allowance is applied once to the combined net volume.

Rectangular volume = Length × Width × Depth × Quantity
Round volume = π × (Diameter ÷ 2)² × Height × Quantity
Ring volume = π ÷ 4 × (Outer Diameter² − Inner Diameter²) × Height × Quantity
Order volume = Net volume × (1 + Waste % ÷ 100)
Weight = Order volume × Density
Cubic yards = Cubic metres × 1.3079506193   and   Cubic feet = Cubic metres × 35.3146667215

Supported shapes

  • Slab / pad: rectangular length × width × thickness.
  • Rectangular footing: rectangular length × width × depth.
  • Wall: length × height × thickness.
  • Column / round footing: circular area × height.
  • Circular ring / pipe form: annular area × height, with inner diameter not greater than outer diameter.

Why bag count is optional

A bag's stated yield depends on the product and packaging. ToolVault therefore does not pretend that a fixed bag weight universally equals a fixed finished-concrete volume. Enter the manufacturer's published yield and the calculator rounds the resulting bag count up to a whole bag.
