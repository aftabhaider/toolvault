Rotate PDF

Fix sideways or upside-down pages and save the PDF with the new orientation. Rotate every page or only the ones you choose.

    How to use Rotate PDF

    1. Choose or drop a PDF.
    2. Pick the rotation and whether it applies to every page or only some.
    3. Select Rotate and download.

    Permanent rotation

    The rotation is saved in the PDF, so the pages open the right way up in every viewer and when printed.

    Quality

    Rotating does not re-render anything: text, links and images are unchanged.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I rotate a PDF and save it?

    Choose the PDF, pick 90°, 180° or 270°, and select Rotate and download. The saved file keeps the new orientation.

    Can I rotate only one page?

    Yes. Choose “Selected pages” and type the page number.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The rotation is applied in your browser; the file stays on your device.

    Does rotating reduce quality?

    No. Only the page orientation setting changes; the content is untouched.
