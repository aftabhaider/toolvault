    How to use HEIC to JPG Converter

    1. Choose or drop one or more .heic or .heif photos.
    2. Pick JPG (best for sharing) or PNG.
    3. Select Convert photos. One photo downloads directly; several download as a ZIP.

    What is HEIC?

    HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the photo format iPhones and iPads use by default since iOS 11. It stores photos at about half the size of JPG, but many apps, websites and Windows PCs still cannot open it.

    Privacy

    The photos are decoded in your browser with the open-source heic2any library. The converted files do not carry the original’s embedded metadata, such as the GPS location where a photo was taken.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert HEIC to JPG for free?

    Choose your HEIC photos, keep JPG selected and select Convert photos. There is no sign-up, watermark or limit on the number of photos.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Conversion happens on your device; the photos never leave it.

    Can I stop my iPhone saving HEIC?

    Yes: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible saves new photos as JPG. Photos you already took stay HEIC, which is what this converter is for.

    Is the quality reduced?

    JPG at High quality is visually identical for normal viewing. Choose PNG if you need a lossless copy.
