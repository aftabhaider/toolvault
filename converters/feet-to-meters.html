Feet to Meters Converter

Convert feet to metres or metres to feet. One foot is exactly 0.3048 metres, so one metre is about 3.28084 feet.

How to convert feet to metres

metres = feet × 0.3048

feet = metres ÷ 0.3048 (≈ metres × 3.28084)

Worked example. A 12 ft room is 12 × 0.3048 = 3.6576 m long. A 5 m length is 5 ÷ 0.3048 = 16.4042 ft.

About the units

Foot (ft, ′)
Exactly 0.3048 m, or 12 inches. Used for room sizes, heights and altitudes in the US and in aviation worldwide.
Metre (m)
The SI base unit of length, used for distances and building dimensions almost everywhere outside the US.

Frequently asked questions

How many metres are in a foot?

Exactly 0.3048 metres.

How many feet are in a metre?

One metre is 3.28084 feet.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply square feet by 0.09290304 (that is 0.3048 squared). For room areas, use the square footage calculator.

Is 3 feet a metre?

No. 3 ft is 0.9144 m, about 8.6 cm short of a metre.
