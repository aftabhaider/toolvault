How to convert centimetres to inches

inches = centimetres ÷ 2.54

centimetres = inches × 2.54

Worked example. To convert 30 cm to inches, divide by 2.54: 30 ÷ 2.54 = 11.811 in. To go back, multiply: 12 in × 2.54 = 30.48 cm.

About the units

Centimetre (cm)
One hundredth of a metre, the SI unit of length. Used for everyday lengths and body measurements in most of the world.
Inch (in, ″)
Defined since the 1959 international yard and pound agreement as exactly 2.54 cm. Used in the US and, informally, in the UK for heights, screens and DIY.

Frequently asked questions

How many inches are in a centimetre?

One centimetre is 0.3937 inches (1 ÷ 2.54).

How many centimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimetres. This is a legal definition, not a rounded measurement, so 1 in = 2.54 cm with no error.

How do I convert cm to feet and inches?

Divide the centimetres by 30.48 to get feet; the whole number is the feet. Convert the remainder back to inches by dividing it by 2.54. For heights, use the height converter linked below, which does both steps.

How do I convert 100 cm to inches?

100 ÷ 2.54 = 39.3701 inches, or about 39 3/8 inches.
