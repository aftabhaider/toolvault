Liters to Gallons Converter

Convert litres to gallons or gallons to litres. A US gallon (3.785 L) is smaller than a UK imperial gallon (4.546 L); choose the one you need.

How to convert litres to gallons

US gallons = litres ÷ 3.785411784

UK gallons = litres ÷ 4.54609

Worked example. A 50 L fuel tank holds 50 ÷ 3.785411784 = 13.21 US gallons, or 50 ÷ 4.54609 = 11 UK gallons.

About the units

Litre (L)
1,000 cm³. Used for fuel, drinks and tank sizes in most countries.
Gallon (gal)
US gallon: exactly 3.785411784 L (231 cubic inches). UK imperial gallon: exactly 4.54609 L. UK fuel economy (mpg) uses imperial gallons.

Frequently asked questions

How many litres are in a gallon?

3.785 L in a US gallon and 4.546 L in a UK (imperial) gallon.

How many gallons is 1 litre?

0.2642 US gallons or 0.22 UK gallons.

Why are US and UK gallons different?

The US kept the old English wine gallon (231 in³); Britain redefined its gallon in 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. The two have been different ever since.

How many litres is a 55-gallon drum?

A 55 US gallon drum holds 208.2 L.
