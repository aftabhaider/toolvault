Grams to Ounces Converter

Convert grams to ounces or ounces to grams. This is the weight ounce (avoirdupois), used for food and parcels; for liquids, use the ml to fl oz converter.

How to convert grams to ounces

ounces = grams ÷ 28.349523125

grams = ounces × 28.349523125

Worked example. 250 g of flour is 250 ÷ 28.349523125 = 8.82 oz. An 8 oz steak is 8 × 28.349523125 = 226.8 g.

About the units

Gram (g)
One thousandth of a kilogram. Recipes outside the US weigh dry ingredients in grams.
Ounce (oz)
The avoirdupois ounce is exactly 28.349523125 g; 16 oz = 1 lb. Not the same as a fluid ounce (volume) or a troy ounce (31.1035 g, used for precious metals).

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in an ounce?

Exactly 28.349523125 grams, usually rounded to 28.35 g (or 28 g in recipes).

How many ounces is 100 grams?

3.527 ounces.

Is a fluid ounce the same as an ounce?

No. A fluid ounce measures volume; an ounce measures weight. One US fl oz of water weighs about 1.04 oz. Use the ml to fl oz converter for liquids.

How many grams are in a pound?

453.59237 grams (16 × 28.349523125).
