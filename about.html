About ToolVault

Learn what ToolVault is, how its calculators and online tools are built, and the principles used for accuracy, usefulness and simple user experiences.

A universal online toolbox

ToolVault is a growing library of free calculators, converters, generators and practical online tools. The goal is simple: help people calculate, convert, plan, analyze and complete everyday or professional tasks from one place.

Built for people first

Each tool is designed to make the main task clear before the supporting explanations. Calculators aim to expose the relevant formula, units, assumptions and worked examples so users can understand the result rather than simply receive a number.

Accuracy matters

Calculation tools are developed around explicit formulas, unit normalization, full-precision internal calculations, display-only rounding and numerical checks. Results that depend on supplier data, weather forecasts or project assumptions are labeled as estimates.

Global by design

ToolVault is designed for users worldwide. Wherever possible, tools support common metric and imperial units and avoid assumptions that only make sense in one country.
