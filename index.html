About ToolVault

ToolVault is being built as a universal online toolbox rather than a single-purpose calculator site. The long-term goal is to help people calculate, convert, generate, analyze, plan and complete tasks from one fast, accessible place.

Tools will be designed for global use wherever possible, with support for different units, currencies, formats and practical requirements. Localized versions can be added later when a calculation depends on country-specific rules or data.
