How the Gravel Calculator Works

For each project area, ToolVault calculates the geometric volume from the shape and dimensions, adds the waste allowance, and estimates weight using the density you enter. Multiple areas can be combined into one project.

Volume = Area × Depth   •   Volume with waste = Volume × (1 + waste ÷ 100)   •   Weight = Volume with waste × density

Why the density matters

Gravel can be sold by tonnes, kilograms, cubic yards, cubic metres or bags. Density varies by aggregate type and condition, so using the supplier's stated density will usually give a better purchasing estimate.

Where can you use it?

  • Driveways, parking areas and paths
  • Drainage, landscaping and decorative gravel
  • Sub-base and foundation planning
  • Projects with several separate areas

Metric vs. imperial inputs

Metric mode uses metres for length and width and centimetres for depth. Imperial mode uses feet plus optional inches for length and width, and inches for depth. Results are also shown in cubic metres, cubic yards and cubic feet.
