Privacy Policy

ToolVault privacy information covering general website use, calculator inputs, analytics and third-party services.

General approach

ToolVault is designed to provide browser-based calculators and online utilities. Many calculations run directly in your browser, and ordinary calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to a server unless a particular tool needs an external service.

Third-party services

Some tools may use third-party services, such as location or weather data, to provide a requested feature. Those requests are subject to the applicable third party's privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

ToolVault may add privacy-respecting analytics or other measurement services in the future. Any such service should be disclosed here when enabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the ToolVault contact page.
