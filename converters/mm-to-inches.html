How to convert millimetres to inches

inches = millimetres ÷ 25.4

millimetres = inches × 25.4

Worked example. A 19 mm board is 19 ÷ 25.4 = 0.748 in, which is 3/4″ to the nearest 1/16 inch.

About the units

Millimetre (mm)
One thousandth of a metre. The standard unit on engineering drawings, tools and building products outside the US.
Inch (in, ″)
Exactly 25.4 mm. US tape measures divide it into halves, quarters, eighths and sixteenths.

Frequently asked questions

How many mm are in an inch?

Exactly 25.4 millimetres.

How do I convert mm to a fraction of an inch?

Divide by 25.4 to get decimal inches, multiply the decimal part by 16 and round to the nearest whole number; that is the number of sixteenths. Simplify the fraction where you can (8/16 = 1/2).

What is 1/8 inch in mm?

1/8 in = 0.125 × 25.4 = 3.175 mm.

Is 10 mm the same as 3/8 inch?

Not exactly: 10 mm is 0.3937 in, while 3/8 in is 9.525 mm. They are often used interchangeably for tools, but check the fit for precise work.
