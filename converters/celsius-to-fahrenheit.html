Celsius to Fahrenheit Converter

Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit or Fahrenheit to Celsius. Unlike length or weight, temperature needs an offset as well as a ratio, so use the full formula, not a single factor.

°C
=
°F

25 °C = 77 °F

How to convert degrees Celsius to degrees Fahrenheit

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Worked example. 25 °C × 9/5 = 45, and 45 + 32 = 77 °F. An oven at 350 °F is (350 − 32) × 5/9 = 176.7 °C.

About the units

Degree Celsius (°C)
Water freezes at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C at sea level. Used for weather and cooking almost everywhere outside the US.
Degree Fahrenheit (°F)
Water freezes at 32 °F and boils at 212 °F. Used for weather, ovens and body temperature in the US.

Frequently asked questions

What is 37 °C in Fahrenheit?

98.6 °F, normal body temperature.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

−40. −40 °C is exactly −40 °F.

Is there a quick way to estimate °F from °C?

Double the Celsius figure and add 30. It is close for everyday weather (20 °C → 70 °F, exact 68 °F) but drifts further off at oven temperatures, so use the exact formula for cooking.

What is 180 °C in Fahrenheit for baking?

356 °F, often written as 350 °F on US recipes.
