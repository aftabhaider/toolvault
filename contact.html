Contact ToolVault

Contact ToolVault about calculator accuracy, tool suggestions, technical issues, corrections and general feedback.

Feedback and corrections

ToolVault welcomes reports about calculation errors, unclear labels, broken links, accessibility issues and useful new tool ideas.

What to include

For a calculation issue, include the tool name, the inputs you used, the result you expected and—when possible—the formula or reference you used. This makes technical review much faster.

Availability

A public email address will be added here when ToolVault establishes its preferred support channel. Until then, this page provides the official contact point for the site.
