Image Converter: PNG, JPG and WebP

Convert images to JPG, PNG or WebP. Use it for PNG to JPG, JPG to PNG, WebP to JPG or any other combination; GIF, BMP and AVIF files can be converted too.

    How to use Image Converter: PNG, JPG and WebP

    1. Choose or drop one or more images.
    2. Pick the format you need.
    3. Select Convert images. One image downloads directly; several download as a ZIP.

    Which format should I use?

    JPG for photos you want to share or upload; PNG for screenshots, logos and graphics with sharp edges or transparency; WebP for websites, where it is usually the smallest of the three.

    Quality

    JPG and WebP are saved at 92% quality, which is visually identical to the original for normal viewing. PNG is lossless.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert PNG to JPG?

    Choose the PNG, pick JPG and select Convert images. Transparent areas are filled with white because JPG cannot store transparency.

    How do I convert WebP to JPG?

    Choose the WebP image, pick JPG and select Convert images. Use this when an app or website will not accept WebP.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Conversion runs in your browser; the images never leave your device.

    Why was my WebP saved as PNG?

    A few browsers, mostly older Safari versions, cannot create WebP files. The tool tells you when this happens; use Chrome, Edge or Firefox for WebP.
