Height Converter: Feet and Inches to CM

Enter feet and inches to get centimetres, or type centimetres to get feet and inches. Heights are converted with the exact definitions 1 ft = 12 in and 1 in = 2.54 cm.

How to convert feet and inches to centimetres

cm = (feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54

total inches = cm ÷ 2.54; feet = whole part of (total inches ÷ 12); inches = the remainder

Worked example. 5 ft 7 in is 5 × 12 + 7 = 67 inches, and 67 × 2.54 = 170.18 cm. In the other direction, 180 cm ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 in = 5 ft 10.9 in.

About the units

Feet and inches
1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm. Height is usually written 5′ 7″ or 5 ft 7 in in the US and UK.
Centimetre (cm)
Used for height on passports, medical records and clothing sizes in most countries.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is 5 foot 7 in cm?

5′ 7″ is 170.18 cm.

What is 170 cm in feet and inches?

170 cm is 5 ft 6.9 in.

How many cm is 6 feet?

6 feet is exactly 182.88 cm.

Why is my converted height a decimal?

Centimetres do not divide evenly into inches (1 in = 2.54 cm), so most heights convert to a decimal. Round to the nearest whole centimetre or half inch for everyday use.
