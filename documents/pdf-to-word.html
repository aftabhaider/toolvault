PDF to Word Converter

Convert selectable text from a PDF into an editable Microsoft Word DOCX document. Choose a PDF, extract its text page by page, then download your Word file. Your PDF is processed locally in your browser.

Best for text-based PDFs. Scanned documents require OCR and are not converted by this tool.

Privacy note

The selected PDF is read and converted in this browser; this page does not upload it to ToolVault. PDF.js and the DOCX generation library load from third-party CDNs, so an internet connection is needed to load the tools.

Convert PDF to Word online for free

This PDF to DOCX converter extracts selectable text from each PDF page and places it into an editable Word document. It is useful when you need to reuse text from reports, notes, letters, or simple documents.

How to convert PDF to Word

  • Select a PDF file from your device.
  • Choose Convert to Word and wait while the pages are processed.
  • Download the generated DOCX file and open it in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor.

Important conversion limits

This is a text-focused conversion, not a pixel-perfect layout reconstruction. Columns, tables, images, fonts, page positioning, headers, footers, and complex formatting may not be preserved. Scanned PDFs that contain page images rather than selectable text need OCR first. Password-protected, damaged, or unusually large PDFs may fail.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF to Word converter free?
Yes. You can use it without creating an account.

Are my documents uploaded?
The selected PDF is processed in your browser and is not sent to ToolVault by this page. Supporting libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?
Not directly. Scanned pages need optical character recognition (OCR) before their text can be extracted.

Will the Word document look exactly like the PDF?
No. This tool focuses on extracting text into editable paragraphs. Complex page layouts and visual elements may change or be omitted.

Why is the output empty?
The PDF may contain scanned images instead of selectable text, or its text layer may be unsupported. Try selecting and copying text from the PDF in a reader to check whether a text layer exists.
