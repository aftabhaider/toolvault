Privacy note

Your PDF is processed in this browser and is not uploaded to a ToolVault conversion API. On the first online visit, the browser downloads and caches the open-source conversion libraries. Optional OCR downloads its engine and English language data the first time it is used. These are software assets; your PDF is not sent to those providers. After setup, the core converter can work offline in this browser.

How to convert PDF to Word

  • Select a PDF file from your device.
  • Choose a conversion mode. Optionally enable English OCR for scanned pages.
  • Convert in your browser and download the generated DOCX when it is ready.
  • Open the DOCX in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor and check layout and OCR text.

Important conversion limits

After its first online setup, the local engine supports PDFs up to 60 MB and 150 pages per conversion. Hybrid mode masks selectable text from a rendered page and places editable text over the page background; alignment and font differences can remain. Reflowed editable mode reconstructs selectable text and Word tables, while columns, complex tables, rotated text, forms, unusual fonts and graphics may not match the original. Exact appearance mode embeds high-resolution full-page images and is not individually editable. Hybrid mode keeps artwork as an image and adds editable text over it; this is a best-effort compromise, not a fully native Word reconstruction. Optional English OCR may misread characters, names, dates, decimal points or amounts. Review important documents manually.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF to Word converter free?
Yes. You can use it without creating an account.

Are my documents uploaded?
Your PDF is processed locally. Initial setup downloads the required open-source libraries and caches them under this site's origin. Optional OCR downloads its engine and English language model when first used. The PDF itself is not sent to a conversion API.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?
Yes, with the optional English OCR setting. OCR runs in your browser and may take longer; accuracy varies, so review the converted text.

How do I keep the same layout, logo, header, footer and fonts?
Choose Keep original layout + editable text to combine a high-resolution page background with editable text overlays. Logos and table rules remain part of the background artwork; editing may affect alignment. Choose Exact PDF appearance for a non-editable visual copy.

Can I preserve the layout and edit all text?
Not reliably for every file. Hybrid mode keeps the page design as a high-resolution background and recreates selectable text over it. Text can be edited, but table borders, fills and logos are still background artwork. Exact appearance mode preserves the page as an image. For exact documents, compare the output with the PDF and correct it in Word.

Why is the output empty?
The PDF may be scanned or contain no usable text layer. Enable the optional English OCR setting and retry. OCR can misread content, so check the output.
