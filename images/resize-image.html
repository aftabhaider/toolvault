Resize Image

Change the pixel size of one image or many at once. Resize by percentage, to a set width or height, or to fit inside a box; the proportions are always kept.

    How to use Resize Image

    1. Choose or drop one or more images.
    2. Pick how to resize: a percentage, a width, a height, or a box the image must fit inside.
    3. Select Resize images. Each image keeps its format; several download as a ZIP.

    Keeping proportions

    With Width or Height, the other side is calculated from the original aspect ratio, so the image is never stretched. Fit in box shrinks or enlarges the image until it just fits inside the width and height you give.

    Common sizes

    1920 px wide suits full-screen web images, 1080 × 1080 px suits Instagram posts, and 1200 × 630 px suits link previews on social media.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I resize an image without losing quality?

    Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Enlarging beyond its original size cannot add detail, so use percentages above 100% sparingly.

    Can I resize many images at once?

    Yes. Add several images; they are all resized with the same setting and downloaded as a ZIP.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Resizing runs in your browser; the images stay on your device.

    What format will the resized image be?

    The same as the original: JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG, WebP stays WebP. Other formats such as GIF or BMP are saved as JPG.
