How to convert miles to kilometres

kilometres = miles × 1.609344

miles = kilometres ÷ 1.609344 (≈ km × 0.621371)

Worked example. A 60 mph speed limit is 60 × 1.609344 = 96.56 km/h. A 10 km run is 10 ÷ 1.609344 = 6.214 miles.

About the units

Mile (mi)
The international (statute) mile is exactly 1,609.344 m, or 5,280 ft. Road distances and speed limits in the US and UK use miles.
Kilometre (km)
1,000 m. Used for road distances in most other countries and for race distances (5K, 10K).

Frequently asked questions

How many km are in a mile?

Exactly 1.609344 kilometres.

How many miles is 5K?

3.107 miles.

How far is a marathon in miles?

The official marathon distance is 42.195 km, which is 26.219 miles (usually quoted as 26.2).

Is a nautical mile the same?

No. A nautical mile is exactly 1.852 km, used at sea and in aviation. This converter uses the statute mile used on roads.
