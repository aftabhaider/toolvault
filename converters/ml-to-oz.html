ML to OZ Converter

Convert millilitres to fluid ounces or back. Choose US or UK fluid ounces: they differ by about 4%, so the right choice matters for recipes and medicine.

250 mL = 8.453506 US fl oz

How to convert millilitres to fluid ounces

US fl oz = ml ÷ 29.5735295625

UK fl oz = ml ÷ 28.4130625

Worked example. A 330 ml can is 330 ÷ 29.5735 = 11.16 US fl oz, or 330 ÷ 28.4131 = 11.61 UK fl oz.

About the units

Millilitre (mL)
One thousandth of a litre (1 cm³). Used on bottles, cans and medicine worldwide.
Fluid ounce (fl oz)
US: exactly 29.5735295625 mL (1/128 US gallon). UK: exactly 28.4130625 mL (1/160 imperial gallon).

Frequently asked questions

How many ml are in a fluid ounce?

29.5735 mL in a US fluid ounce and 28.4131 mL in a UK fluid ounce.

How many ounces is 500 ml?

16.91 US fl oz, or 17.6 UK fl oz.

Why do US nutrition labels say 1 fl oz = 30 mL?

US food labelling rules round the fluid ounce to 30 mL for nutrition panels. The exact US fluid ounce is 29.5735 mL; the difference matters only for precise measurement.

How many ml is a cup?

A US cup is 236.588 mL (8 US fl oz). Metric cups in Australia, Canada and the UK are often 250 mL.
