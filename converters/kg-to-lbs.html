KG to LBS Converter

Convert kilograms to pounds or pounds to kilograms. The pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg, so 1 kg is about 2.20462 lb.

How to convert kilograms to pounds

pounds = kilograms ÷ 0.45359237 (≈ kg × 2.20462)

kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237

Worked example. 70 kg ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.32 lb. In UK stones and pounds that is 11 st 0.3 lb (1 stone = 14 lb).

About the units

Kilogram (kg)
The SI unit of mass, used for body weight and goods in most of the world.
Pound (lb)
Exactly 0.45359237 kg (avoirdupois pound). Used for body weight in the US; UK weights are often given in stones and pounds.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is 2.20462 pounds.

How many kg is 1 pound?

Exactly 0.45359237 kg, about 454 grams.

What is 70 kg in stone?

70 kg is 11.023 stone, or 11 st 0.3 lb.

Why is pounds written as lbs?

“lb” comes from the Latin libra. “lbs” is a common informal plural; both mean the same unit.
