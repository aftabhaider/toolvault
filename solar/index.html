Solar PV Planning Tools

ToolVault's solar calculators are designed to provide practical planning estimates for residential, commercial and other photovoltaic installations. Each calculator focuses on a different part of solar system planning.

1. Calculate your load Estimate appliance consumption and determine suitable PV, inverter and battery requirements.
2. Estimate solar generation Use location-based weather and solar-radiation data to estimate expected PV energy generation.
3. Check installation area Estimate the panel surface and practical space required for installing the selected number of solar panels.

How to choose the right solar calculator

Start with the Solar Load Calculator when you want to understand your energy consumption and basic system requirements. Use the Solar Energy Generation Calculator when you want to estimate PV output based on location and weather conditions. Use the Solar Panel Area Calculator when you need to estimate the physical roof or installation space required for your panels.

Solar calculator results are planning estimates

Solar production and system-sizing results can vary because of weather, shading, panel orientation, temperature, equipment specifications, wiring losses, battery characteristics and site conditions. ToolVault calculators are intended for planning and estimation and should not replace a detailed site-specific engineering design.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can a solar calculator help me calculate?

Solar calculators can help estimate electrical load, PV capacity, inverter requirements, battery capacity, solar energy generation and the installation area needed for solar panels.

How do I calculate solar panel area?

Multiply the physical length and width of one panel to find its surface area, then multiply by the number of panels. A practical layout factor can be added to estimate additional installation and access space.

How is solar energy generation estimated?

Solar generation depends on installed PV capacity and available solar irradiation. Weather conditions, panel orientation, tilt and system losses can also affect the estimated output.

Are ToolVault solar calculator results exact?

No. The calculators provide planning estimates. Actual solar system performance depends on equipment, site conditions, weather and the final system design.
